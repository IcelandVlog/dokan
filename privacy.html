Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Dokan is a demo online shop. This policy explains what information the site collects, how it is used, and where it is stored. Dokan is a demo: no real payments are taken and orders are not shipped.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign up with email, your password is handled by Supabase Authentication and is not readable by us. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Order details: the name, phone number, shipping address and payment method you enter at checkout, plus the items and totals of each order.
  • On your device: your cart and theme choice (dark or light) are stored in your browser's local storage.

2. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your account.
  • To record your orders and show them in your order history.
  • To let the store team view and confirm orders and manage stock.

3. Where data is stored and who processes it

Account, product and order data is stored in a Supabase database. The site itself is hosted on Vercel. Google processes sign-in when you choose "Continue with Google". We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

Account and order data is kept until you ask us to delete it, or until the demo store is closed.

5. Your choices

You can sign out at any time. You can clear your cart and theme by clearing this site's data in your browser. To ask for your account or order data to be deleted, contact us using the address below.

6. Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
